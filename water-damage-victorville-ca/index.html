When water is spreading in Victorville

Water damage in Victorville tends to arrive as an ordinary household failure. A supply line splits, a water heater leaks through its base, a washer hose lets go or an air conditioner drain overflows into a ceiling. Whatever the source, the clock starts when the water does, and dispatch is open 24/7 for Victorville callers.

Victorville is the largest city in the Victor Valley, with a ZIP-based population of about 150,900 across three standard ZIP codes. A big city means a lot of homes, and it also means a crew has to know which side of town you are on. Give the dispatcher your street and your ZIP so the route is right the first time.

Our crews come from Apple Valley, which borders Victorville to the east. The two cities are linked by the roads and neighborhoods on either side of the Mojave River, and we dispatch to Victorville for water mitigation, extraction, cleanup and repair.

If water is near electrical outlets or the panel

Stay out of the room. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas concern before you call our line, and shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Stop the source

Shut the main water valve or the fixture valve. If the source is a roof opening, cover it with a tarp if it is safe.

Make the room safe

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground.

Lift and protect

Raise valuables, paper goods and small furniture off wet floors and out of the water path.

Keep clear of dirty water

Keep people and pets out of water from drains or toilets, and do not run fans on it.

Utilities and local authorities in Victorville

The City of Victorville runs its own Water Department, with separate pages for water supply, water quality, wastewater treatment and recycled water. City hall is on Civic Drive, and the department can answer questions about your meter and service line.

The sewer story has a twist. The Victor Valley Wastewater Reclamation Authority treatment plant that takes wastewater from Apple Valley is located in Victorville, according to the Town of Apple Valley utilities element. For a sewage backup, ask the City of Victorville which agency maintains the sewer line near your home before arranging cleanup.

Permit and inspection questions for rebuild work go to the city, not to the Town of Apple Valley. Each city has its own building rules, so ask Victorville before you open walls beyond drywall and flooring.

What we do for Victorville homes

Most Victorville jobs begin with emergency water removal to pump out standing water, followed by water mitigation to dry the structure. After that, water damage cleanup removes ruined materials, and water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and finishes.
