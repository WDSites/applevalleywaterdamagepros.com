Summer, winter and storm season in Adelanto

Water damage in Adelanto follows the seasons. In summer, air conditioners run for long stretches, and condensate lines clog and spill into ceilings and closets. In winter, cold nights threaten exposed pipes in garages and outside walls. After the rare big storm, roofs and block walls show every weakness.

That pattern is why callers in Adelanto reach us for a mix of problems. A ceiling stain in August, a split pipe in January and a wet garage after a storm all call for the same first move, which is to stop the source and call the 24/7 line.

First steps while you wait

Shut off the main water valve, lift valuables off the floor, turn off power to wet areas only if it is safe and keep people and pets away from dirty water. Call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk before you call us.

If water came up from a drain or a toilet, stay out of it and leave fans off. Adelanto sewage calls have a clear first contact, which is the city utility.

Adelanto runs its own water and sewer, and that helps in an emergency

The City of Adelanto Water Department provides water and wastewater service to residents and businesses in the city, and about 99 percent of residents receive water from the public supply, according to the city. The Public Utilities Authority and the Director of Public Utilities oversee the system.

For sewage, the city operates an activated sludge wastewater treatment facility under a contract with PERC Water Corporation. PERC also handles routine cleaning of the collection system and sewage spill response. That makes the first call simple for a street-level backup, which is the City of Adelanto Utility Billing and Water Department, and the second call is ours for cleanup inside the house.

Keep your account information handy. The city requires an account holder to request changes, and having your name and address on hand speeds up any service call to the utility.
